⚡ How to Upgrade

A new version of {{ docker_image }} is available on Docker Hub. Upgrading takes about 30 seconds — no reinstallation needed.

1

Pull the latest image

docker pull {{ docker_image }}:latest
2

Stop and remove the current container

docker stop ev-report
docker rm ev-report

With docker compose instead: docker compose down

3

Start the updated container

docker run -d -p 8000:8000 \
  -e DJANGO_SECRET_KEY=changeme \
  --name ev-report \
  {{ docker_image }}:latest

With docker compose instead: docker compose up -d

4

Open the app

Visit http://localhost:8000 — your previous uploads are not affected.

💡 Tip: Running Watchtower alongside this container handles upgrades automatically.

📖 User Manual

Getting started

Export your charging history from your UniFi EV Station console as a CSV file, then upload it on the home page. The app parses it instantly and brings you to this dashboard.

Navigating months

Use the month buttons at the top to switch between months. Only months present in your data appear. The daily kWh chart and day table update when you switch. The two monthly charts (cost and kWh totals) always show all-time data.

Changing the kWh price

Type a new price in the kWh Price ($) field and press Apply (or hit Enter). Every cost figure recalculates immediately. Default rate is $0.121/kWh.

Charts

  • Daily kWh Consumed — energy used per day for the selected month
  • Cost by Month — total cost per month across your entire history
  • Total kWh by Month — total energy per month across your entire history

Hover over any bar or point to see the exact value.

Day table

Shows every charging day in the selected month: date, day of week, sessions, kWh, charge time, total session time, daily cost, and running cumulative cost for the month.

Exporting

  • ⬇ Excel — full workbook with one sheet per month, grand summary, and an editable kWh price cell (B2 on Dashboard) that recalculates everything in Excel too.
  • ⬇ PDF — formatted multi-page report, ready to print or share.

Both exports use the kWh price currently set in the app.

Updates

When a new version is released, a green banner appears at the top of this page. Click How to upgrade → for step-by-step Docker instructions.
